How we approach market entry

  1. Understand before acting

    We look at the market, your offer, your current position and the commercial context before deciding where the work should focus.

  2. Adapt where it matters

    Positioning, localization, sales material and channels should reflect how the market actually buys, not just what worked somewhere else.

  3. Target with a reason

    We do not build lists for the sake of volume. Buyers, distributors and partners should be relevant to the offer and the route you are trying to build.

  4. Learn from the market

    Responses, objections and results are useful information. We use them to improve the message, targeting and commercial approach.

Start with the part that matters most.

Some companies need stronger positioning. Others need localization, a clearer channel strategy, better sales material or a more focused route to buyers and distributors.

We focus the work around the part of the market entry that is most likely to improve progress.

More than a contact list

Every target should have a reason behind it.

Buyer and distributor research starts with defining the right target profile. We look at fit, relevance and commercial context, then prioritise the companies that make the most sense to approach.

The goal is not to hand over a long list of names. It is to build a focused set of companies with a clear reason for why each one belongs there, then use market response to improve the targeting and approach.

Practical implementation

Make the offer work in the market.

Depending on the route, the work may involve website localization, sales materials, product pages, marketplace listings, terminology, trust signals or local commercial messaging.

Ligeza can support the practical work around market entry, including research, content adaptation, account targeting, outreach preparation, commercial coordination and pipeline structure.

Where the project requires legal, customs, freight, VAT or product compliance expertise, we bring in or recommend the relevant specialist rather than pretending to cover everything in house.
