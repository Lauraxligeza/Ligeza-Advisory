Built for practical international expansion.

Ligeza Advisory helps companies prepare for new markets, adapt their commercial approach and turn expansion plans into practical market entry work.

The focus is on the parts that directly affect progress: positioning, localization, channels, buyers, distributors, sales materials and early market development.

The work is kept focused and practical. Research is used to support decisions, but the goal is always to help move the business closer to operating effectively in the market.

How Ligeza works

Commercial before theoretical

Research should lead to a decision or action, not simply produce a report.

Adapt to the market

What works in one country may not work in another. Positioning, language, channels and sales material should reflect the market being entered.

Focus on relevance

Whether researching buyers, distributors or channels, the aim is to identify what genuinely fits rather than maximise volume.

Know where specialist expertise begins

Legal, customs, tax, freight and product compliance work is handled by the appropriate specialist when required.
