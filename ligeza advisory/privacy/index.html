Privacy Policy

At Ligeza Advisory, we respect your privacy and handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have regarding your personal data.

We process personal information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Dutch privacy legislation.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information in the following circumstances.

When you contact us

If you contact Ligeza Advisory by email, we may collect your name, email address, company information and any other details you voluntarily provide.

When you become a client

We may collect additional information necessary to prepare proposals, communicate with you, provide agreed services and maintain business records.

When conducting business research

As part of our international business development activities, we may process professional contact information obtained from company websites, business directories, trade fair listings, supplier advertisements and other relevant business sources.

This may include names, professional contact details, job titles, company information and relevant business interests.

When you visit our website

Our website hosting provider may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information and access logs, to deliver the website and maintain its security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and communicating with potential clients.
  • Preparing proposals and delivering agreed services.
  • Maintaining existing business relationships.
  • Conducting relevant business research and identifying potential commercial contacts.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.
  • Fulfilling applicable legal and administrative obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, our legal basis for processing personal information may be our legitimate business interests, taking steps towards a contract, performing a contract or complying with a legal obligation.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider those interests alongside the rights and interests of the individuals concerned.

Any commercial outreach is also subject to applicable electronic communications and marketing legislation.

4. Where Your Information Comes From

Most personal information is provided directly by individuals who contact us or engage our services.

Where relevant to our business development activities, we may also obtain professional information from publicly available business sources, commercial directories and supplier listings.

When we obtain information indirectly, we provide the information required by applicable data protection law.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support our business operations, including our website hosting and business email providers.

Business email provider: [To be confirmed]

Where necessary, information may also be shared with professional service providers or specialists involved in delivering agreed services.

We limit information sharing to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

We will confirm whether our service providers transfer personal information outside the European Economic Area and identify any applicable safeguards before finalising this policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

As a proposed retention policy, general enquiries that do not result in a business relationship will be retained for up to 12 months following the last relevant communication.

Business prospecting records will be reviewed regularly and deleted when they are no longer relevant or necessary.

Client and financial records may be retained for longer periods where required by applicable law or necessary to resolve contractual matters.

We regularly review the personal information we hold and delete information that is no longer required.

7. Website Analytics and Cookies

Ligeza Advisory does not currently use Microsoft Clarity or other advertising and behavioural analytics tools.

Website fonts are hosted locally rather than being requested directly from an external font provider.

Our hosting provider may process technical access logs necessary for website delivery, security and maintenance.

We will update this policy if we introduce additional analytics, cookies or other technologies that process visitor information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Request restrictions on how your information is processed.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including relevant direct marketing activities.
  • Request the transfer of your information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise your rights by contacting:

contact@ligezaadvisory.com

We will review your request and respond within the period required by applicable data protection law.

You also have the right to submit a complaint to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

9. Information Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse and inappropriate disclosure.

Access to personal information is limited according to business needs.

No method of electronic communication or information storage is entirely risk free.

10. Automated Decision Making

Ligeza Advisory does not use automated decision making that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning individuals.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business activities, service providers or legal obligations.

The latest version will be made available on this website, together with its most recent update date.
